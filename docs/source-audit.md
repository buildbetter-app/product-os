## What each source does best

### mekenthompson/ProductOS

Strongest at durable artifacts, hierarchy, evidence receipts, fresh-process review, change control, and connecting customer jobs to delivery. Its eight skills form a coherent system rather than a loose catalog. Product OS adopts the emphasis on durable outcomes and review gates, while avoiding source-specific file hierarchies and overly long single skills.

### assimovt/productskills

Strongest compact baseline across the common PM lifecycle: interviews, research synthesis, problem validation, opportunity mapping, strategy, prioritization, experiments, metrics, scope, roadmap, launch, and positioning. Product OS retains this lifecycle completeness while adding evidence provenance, decision states, safety boundaries, and BuildBetter acceleration.

### deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills

Strongest breadth in facilitation, lifecycle, end-of-life, competitive intelligence, financial framing, stakeholder work, and career-level advisory material. Many skills are several hundred lines and the CC BY-NC-SA license conflicts with an MIT commercial pack. Product OS uses its catalog as a topic gap checklist only and independently authors overlapping workflows.

### RefoundAI/lenny-skills

Strongest expert perspective across PM craft, product taste, reviews, AI product work, product-market fit, growth, leadership, and organization design. The skills are concise but often principle-oriented rather than deterministic. Product OS uses them as expert influences while adding executable workflows, outputs, and quality gates.

### phuryn/pm-skills

Strongest task-shaped operational prompts and practical artifacts across discovery, execution, market research, strategy, analytics, launch, and AI-assisted shipping. Product OS keeps the actionable orientation while removing tool-specific placeholders, tightening evidence rules, and consolidating duplicate names.

## Selection method

Each candidate was evaluated on:

- common PM trigger and durable user value;
- ability to produce a decision or reusable artifact;
- evidence discipline and resistance to hallucinated certainty;
- distinctness from neighboring skills;
- safety around customer-facing or irreversible actions;
- portability across agent harnesses;
- opportunity for BuildBetter evidence or MCP acceleration.

The result is 45 canonical skills rather than a union of hundreds of source skills. The second catalog wave adds market segmentation, goals, instrumentation, experiment analysis, retention, onboarding, go-to-market motion, business cases, portfolio management, and a focused AI Product pack. Career coaching, founder psychology, generic communications, legal drafting, SQL, resume review, and other useful but non-core topics remain outside Product OS.

## BuildBetter MCP coverage

The current BuildBetter MCP surface was grouped into product workflows:

- organization Skillsets;
- calls, transcripts, people, accounts, documents, and knowledge pages;
- signals, structured extractions, properties, tags, and aggregates;
- projects, triage, linked tickets, and project types;
- Smart Tag drafting, evaluation, review, publishing, and backfills;
- synthetic personas, companies, panels, studies, and chats;
- survey authoring, delivery, intercepts, responses, and settings;
- knowledge gaps and release-readiness analysis;
- portal, changelog, call-prep, agent-session, success, and sales surfaces as deployed.

Generic Product OS skills use only capability-level acceleration. Exact mutating workflows belong in BuildBetter-exclusive BB-Skills and must be validated against the registered production tool inventory before customer distribution.
