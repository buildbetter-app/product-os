# Align Product Stakeholders

## Contract

Aim for decision clarity and durable commitment, not unanimous enthusiasm. Surface incentives, constraints, and disagreements early. Never attribute a position to someone without evidence.

## Workflow

1. Define the decision, scope, deadline, decision owner, contributors, consulted parties, and affected groups.
2. Map each stakeholder's goals, incentives, risks, authority, knowledge, dependencies, preferred evidence, and current position.
3. Identify true conflicts: goals, facts, risk tolerance, sequencing, resources, or unclear rights.
4. Gather missing evidence and distinguish resolvable factual disputes from value or priority choices.
5. Choose engagement: inform, consult, co-create, negotiate, or escalate. Sequence conversations to reduce surprise without forming hidden coalitions.
6. Prepare a pre-read with decision, context, options, tradeoffs, recommendation, dissent, and explicit asks.
7. Record the decision, owner, commitments, unresolved dissent, communication plan, and review trigger.

## BuildBetter Acceleration

If BuildBetter MCP is available, search organization skills first. Use documents, knowledge pages, project evidence, calls, and linked artifacts to establish prior decisions and customer impact. Do not infer private motivations from CRM or conversation data.

## Output

Return stakeholder map, decision rights, conflict diagnosis, evidence gaps, engagement sequence, pre-read outline, meeting decision frame, commitments, dissent, and communication plan.

## Quality Gate

Reject broad labels such as resistant or supportive without evidence, meetings with no decision, and alignment plans that hide tradeoffs or omit affected customers.
