# Design Go To Market Motion

## Contract

Go-to-market is the repeatable system by which customers discover, evaluate, buy, adopt, and expand—not the launch event or messaging document. Match the motion to buying reality, product value, economics, and organizational capability.

## Workflow

1. Define the beachhead segment, job, trigger, buyer and user roles, alternatives, proof required, price and contract context, and current stage.
2. Map the customer path from awareness through evaluation, commitment, onboarding, value realization, renewal, and expansion.
3. Compare plausible motions: self-serve, product-led, sales-led, partner-led, community-led, services-led, or hybrid. State assumptions and disqualifiers.
4. Select channels and handoffs based on customer behavior and unit economics, not channel popularity.
5. Define the product, marketing, sales, success, support, operations, and partner capabilities required at each stage.
6. Specify proof assets, qualification, trial or demo, activation, pricing, procurement, implementation, and expansion mechanics.
7. Model conversion, cycle time, acquisition cost, service load, retention, expansion, capacity, and feedback loops using ranges.
8. Create a staged validation plan with owners, measures, guardrails, stop conditions, and the evidence required to scale.

## BuildBetter Acceleration

If BuildBetter MCP is available, search organization skills first. Use calls, objections, surveys, account context, signals, documents, projects, and customer outcomes to understand buying and adoption. Never send messages, enroll audiences, or change customer access without explicit approval.

## Output

Return target segment, buying journey, motion choice and alternatives, channel system, handoffs, proof, capability gaps, economic model, validation sequence, measures, and scale conditions.

## Quality Gate

Reject motions chosen without buying evidence, funnels with no adoption or retention path, PLG labels for products that require high-touch change, and scale plans whose economics or operating capacity remain unknown.
