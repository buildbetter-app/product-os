# Prioritize Product Opportunities

## Contract

Prioritize customer problems or outcomes before solutions. Scores support a decision; they do not make it. Keep confidence and evidence visible, and compare only items at a similar altitude.

## Workflow

1. State the strategic outcome, capacity constraint, horizon, and decision owner.
2. Normalize candidates into opportunity statements with segment, situation, impact, evidence, and non-goals.
3. Remove duplicates, mandatory work, already committed work, and items too vague to compare; place them in explicit lanes.
4. Select a small set of criteria appropriate to the decision: strategic contribution, customer impact, reach, urgency, evidence strength, risk reduction, effort, dependency, and learning value.
5. Define scales and evidence rules before scoring. Use ranges when inputs are uncertain.
6. Rank, then run sensitivity and portfolio checks for sequencing, concentration, obligations, and option value.
7. Write the decision rationale, tradeoffs, deferrals, and what new evidence would change the order.

## BuildBetter Acceleration

If BuildBetter MCP is available, search organization skills first. Use signal aggregates and evidence rows, surveys, account context, projects, triage items, and linked tickets. Do not promote or mutate project state without explicit user approval.

## Output

Return decision context, normalized opportunities, criteria and weights, evidence-backed comparison, sensitivity, portfolio view, ranked recommendation, deferrals, and change triggers.

## Quality Gate

Reject feature popularity as strategy, fake precision, cross-altitude comparisons, and scoring unsupported by evidence. Show mandatory or risk work separately.
