# Define Product Metrics

## Contract

Metrics exist to change decisions. Tie each measure to a value mechanism and behavior. Distinguish outcomes, leading inputs, health guardrails, diagnostic measures, and business results.

## Workflow

1. State the customer value and product or business decision the metric system supports.
2. Map the causal value loop from eligible user to meaningful outcome.
3. Choose an outcome or North Star measure that reflects delivered value, not mere activity. Define population, event, numerator, denominator, window, and exclusions.
4. Build a small input tree of controllable behaviors that plausibly drive the outcome.
5. Add guardrails for quality, trust, fairness, reliability, cost, and cannibalization.
6. Define segment cuts, baselines, data sources, owners, refresh cadence, and known measurement gaps.
7. Set decision rules and investigation triggers. Separate targets from definitions and document target rationale.
8. Audit for gaming, survivorship, lag, denominator shifts, and vanity metrics.

## BuildBetter Acceleration

If BuildBetter MCP is available, search organization skills first. Use signal and tag aggregates, surveys, projects, account evidence, documents, and knowledge pages to define customer-value measures and leading qualitative indicators. Do not claim product analytics data was queried unless a connected source actually provides it.

## Output

Return metric hierarchy, exact definitions, causal map, data sources, segments, owners, baselines, targets or thresholds, guardrails, cadence, decision rules, and instrumentation gaps.

## Quality Gate

Every metric must have a definition, owner, source, and action. Reject totals without denominators, measures easily optimized without value, and dashboards with no decision path.
