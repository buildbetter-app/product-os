# Plan Product Discovery

## Contract

Plan discovery to retire meaningful uncertainty, not to produce research theater. Separate observed facts, interpretations, assumptions, and decisions. Scale the cadence to the cost and reversibility of the decision.

## Workflow

1. State the decision discovery must enable, its owner, deadline, and cost of being wrong.
2. Inventory current evidence and assumptions. Mark source, date, segment, and confidence; do not convert repetition into truth without independent support.
3. Rank uncertainties by decision impact and evidence weakness. Select the smallest set that could change the decision.
4. Match each uncertainty to a method: interviews for context and causality, observation for behavior, product data for prevalence, prototypes for comprehension or usability, and experiments for causal response.
5. Define participants and sampling. Include the relevant segment, non-users or churned users when useful, and explicit exclusion criteria.
6. Set a weekly operating loop: collect, tag, synthesize, decide, and update the evidence ledger. Name the point at which more research has diminishing value.
7. Produce a discovery backlog ordered by learning value, not stakeholder loudness.

## Output

Return the decision statement, evidence and assumption inventory, ranked learning questions, method and sample plan, cadence, stop conditions, and the next three discovery actions. Every action must name the uncertainty it retires.

## Quality Gate

Reject a plan that lacks a decision, mixes evidence with inference, samples only convenient users, or schedules activity without a learning-to-decision path.
