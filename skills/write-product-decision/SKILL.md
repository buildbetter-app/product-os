# Write Product Decision

## Contract

Record the decision while context is fresh. A decision record explains why, not every meeting detail. Never rewrite history: supersede earlier records and link them.

## Workflow

1. State the decision in one sentence, its date, status, owner, and scope.
2. Summarize the context, constraint, and customer or business outcome that made a decision necessary.
3. List evidence with source, date, and confidence. Mark assumptions and missing information.
4. Describe viable options, including maintain-current-state when relevant.
5. Record the chosen option, rationale, tradeoffs, risks, and what is explicitly not decided.
6. Capture dissent and conditions under which another option would have been better.
7. State consequences, required actions, owners, communication, measures, and reconsideration or expiry triggers.
8. Link affected strategy, requirements, roadmap, launch, project, and prior decisions.

## BuildBetter Acceleration

If BuildBetter MCP is available, search organization skills first. Use project detail, documents, knowledge pages, customer evidence, surveys, calls, and prior artifacts to ground context. A record may cite BuildBetter IDs, but creating or changing records in external systems needs explicit approval.

## Output

Return a concise decision record with metadata, context, evidence, options, decision, rationale, tradeoffs, dissent, consequences, actions, links, and review triggers.

## Quality Gate

Reject records that omit alternatives, state only the outcome of a meeting, hide dissent, or use a vague reconsideration clause such as if things change.
