# Size Product Bet

## Contract

Appetite is a strategic constraint, not an estimate disguised as a deadline. Match investment to evidence, reversibility, upside, downside, and learning value. Do not design the full solution before choosing the bet size.

## Workflow

1. Define the customer outcome, strategic contribution, urgency, and cost of inaction.
2. Classify reversibility and downside. Identify legal, trust, safety, operational, or platform constraints that raise the bar.
3. Assess evidence strength, key unknowns, expected upside, option value, and dependencies.
4. Choose an appetite: probe, small bet, or major bet. State time, team shape, and opportunity cost as ranges or caps.
5. Describe the smallest coherent approach, rabbit holes, no-gos, and what will not fit.
6. Set validation checkpoints, leading signals, circuit breakers, and kill criteria.
7. Record conditions that justify expanding, shrinking, pausing, or abandoning the bet.

## BuildBetter Acceleration

If BuildBetter MCP is available, search organization skills first. Use customer evidence, projects, triage, linked tickets, surveys, documents, and prior decisions to size uncertainty and demand. Keep estimates from engineering or finance identified by source.

## Output

Return outcome, evidence, reversibility, upside and downside, appetite, scope boundaries, team and time cap, risks, checkpoints, kill criteria, and expansion conditions.

## Quality Gate

Reject unbounded strategic bets, precise forecasts unsupported by evidence, and appetites that expand merely because the proposed solution is large.
