# Evaluate Product Market Fit

## Contract

Product-market fit is segment-specific and evidenced by durable behavior, not a launch moment or one survey threshold. Triangulate leading and lagging signals. Separate value, usability, distribution, pricing, and market problems.

## Workflow

1. Define the segment, job, product state, geography, cohort window, and decision.
2. Build a fit model across activation to value, retained behavior, cohort retention, organic pull, depth or frequency, switching behavior, willingness to pay, expansion, support burden, and customer language.
3. Use PMF survey responses as one evidence stream; inspect respondent selection, usage maturity, sample size, and reasons.
4. Compare cohorts and segments. Identify a narrow pocket of strong fit rather than averaging the whole market.
5. Analyze churn, non-consumption, workarounds, stalled adoption, and customers who succeed without the product.
6. Distinguish product-value gaps from onboarding, reliability, price, positioning, or distribution gaps.
7. Give a fit stage and confidence, then specify the next constraint, experiment, and evidence that could change the verdict.

## BuildBetter Acceleration

If BuildBetter MCP is available, search organization skills first. Use calls, signals, survey responses, account and people context, documents, and aggregates. Combine these with authoritative product, retention, billing, and growth data when available; do not imply BuildBetter contains unconnected metrics.

## Output

Return segment and cohort scope, evidence matrix, retention and pull signals, counterevidence, fit pockets, gap diagnosis, stage and confidence, next constraint, and validation plan.

## Quality Gate

Reject company-wide PMF claims from a small enthusiastic sample, aggregate retention without cohort or segment context, and survey thresholds detached from behavior.
