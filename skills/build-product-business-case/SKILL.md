# Build Product Business Case

## Contract

A business case exposes assumptions and choices; it does not manufacture a precise forecast. Compare against the credible counterfactual and include customer, operational, strategic, and risk effects—not revenue alone.

## Workflow

1. Define the investment decision, decision owner, horizon, alternatives, counterfactual, strategic outcome, and cost of delay.
2. Establish the current baseline for customers, usage, revenue or cost, operations, risk, and capacity with sources and dates.
3. Map value drivers and causal links: customer value, acquisition, retention, expansion, price, efficiency, risk reduction, learning, and strategic option value.
4. Estimate one-time and ongoing product, engineering, data, support, sales, compliance, migration, infrastructure, and opportunity costs.
5. Build conservative, expected, and upside scenarios with ranges, timing, dependencies, confidence, and no-double-counting rules.
6. Test sensitivity to the few assumptions that dominate the result. Include downside, reversibility, failure cost, and break-even conditions.
7. Compare alternatives on economics, customer impact, strategic coherence, risk, learning, and organizational capability.
8. Recommend invest, probe, defer, buy, partner, or stop with funding gates, evidence milestones, and reconsideration triggers.

## BuildBetter Acceleration

If BuildBetter MCP is available, search organization skills first. Use account evidence, calls, signals, surveys, projects, documents, and prior decisions to ground demand and impact. Use authoritative finance, billing, usage, and cost systems for numerical baselines.

## Output

Return decision and counterfactual, evidence ledger, value-driver model, cost model, scenarios, sensitivity, risks, alternatives, recommendation, funding gates, and missing evidence.

## Quality Gate

Reject point forecasts without ranges, benefits without causal drivers, omitted operating costs, sunk-cost reasoning, duplicated value, and recommendations whose result flips under a small plausible assumption change without acknowledging it.
