# Plan Outcome Roadmap

## Contract

Roadmap outcomes and decisions, not a feature calendar. Confidence must decrease with distance. Separate commitments, shaped bets, options, obligations, and discovery work.

## Workflow

1. Establish strategic outcomes, constraints, planning horizon, capacity, obligations, and decision cadence.
2. Normalize candidate work into outcomes or customer opportunities; separate mandatory risk or maintenance work.
3. Attach evidence, owner, target segment, leading measure, confidence, dependencies, and key assumptions.
4. Prioritize and sequence by strategic contribution, urgency, learning, dependency, risk, and portfolio balance.
5. Place items into Now, Next, and Later or equivalent horizons with explicit entry criteria. Use dates only for true commitments or constraints.
6. Define discovery and decision gates before moving an item closer.
7. Record deferrals, tradeoffs, capacity allocation, and change triggers. Establish a regular evidence-based review.

## BuildBetter Acceleration

If BuildBetter MCP is available, search organization skills first. Use project types, projects, triage, linked tickets, signals, surveys, documents, and prior decisions. Treat observed tool state as evidence; promotion or project mutation requires explicit approval.

## Output

Return roadmap principles, horizon table, outcome and evidence per item, confidence, measures, dependencies, capacity allocation, obligations lane, deferrals, decision gates, and review cadence.

## Quality Gate

Reject distant feature commitments, items with no outcome or evidence, overloaded Now horizons, and roadmaps that omit maintenance, compliance, or learning capacity.
