# Set Product Goals

## Contract

Goals focus choices; metrics describe reality. Start from strategy and customer value, not from available dashboards. Do not disguise features, projects, or activity counts as outcomes.

## Workflow

1. Establish strategic horizon, governing choices, customer outcome, constraints, decision owner, and current baseline.
2. Identify the few changes in customer behavior or product performance that would demonstrate strategic progress.
3. Write clear objectives that name a direction and meaningful outcome without prescribing a solution.
4. Define measurable results with population, baseline, target or threshold, window, source, owner, and rationale. Separate committed targets from learning goals.
5. Add quality, trust, cost, reliability, and cannibalization guardrails that prevent local optimization.
6. Test controllability and contribution: the team must influence the result without being able to achieve it through low-value gaming.
7. Resolve dependencies, shared ownership, conflicting incentives, and capacity implications.
8. Set check-in cadence, confidence, escalation thresholds, and rules for continuing, revising, or retiring a goal when evidence changes.

## BuildBetter Acceleration

If BuildBetter MCP is available, search organization skills first. Use customer evidence, surveys, project state, documents, prior decisions, and signal aggregates to ground desired outcomes. Combine them with authoritative product and business baselines when connected.

## Output

Return strategy link, objectives, measurable results, baselines, targets, definitions, guardrails, owners, dependencies, confidence, cadence, and change rules.

## Quality Gate

Reject goals that are feature lists, have no baseline or owner, reward activity without value, conflict with strategy, or can be declared achieved without observable customer or business evidence.
