# Shape Product Strategy

## Contract

Strategy is a set of choices under constraint. Distinguish the challenge from goals, the policy from aspirations, and coherent actions from a backlog. Every market or customer claim needs an evidence status.

## Workflow

1. Define the strategic horizon, scope, constraints, and the hard challenge blocking progress.
2. Build the diagnosis from customer, market, product, operational, and economic evidence. Label claims evidenced, hypothesis, or to validate.
3. Identify the advantage or leverage available and the tradeoff it implies.
4. Write a guiding policy that narrows choices and provides decision tests.
5. Choose coherent actions, funded customer jobs, capabilities, and an explicit allocation of attention or capacity.
6. Name what is deferred, stopped, or outside the strategy, with reasons.
7. Define leading indicators, risks, kill or reconsideration signals, and validation work for weak claims.
8. Run a smell test: remove goals disguised as strategy, slogans, mutually incompatible choices, and plans that fund everything.

## BuildBetter Acceleration

If BuildBetter MCP is available, search organization skills first. Use signals, surveys, account and people evidence, documents, knowledge pages, projects, and aggregates. Preserve source IDs and distinguish internal opinion from customer evidence.

## Output

Return diagnosis, guiding policy, coherent actions, allocation, deferrals, decision tests, evidence ledger, risks, indicators, and reconsideration triggers.

## Quality Gate

The strategy must make at least one consequential choice, fund a bounded set of work, and explain why the chosen system could win. If no hard choice exists, report that the document is a plan or set of goals.
