# Design Pricing And Packaging

## Contract

Price the value exchange, not the feature inventory. Separate packaging, value metric, price level, discounting, entitlements, billing operations, and migration. Use current verified data for existing prices and customer economics.

## Workflow

1. Define strategy, best-fit segment, buying roles, job, alternatives, and value created.
2. Gather evidence on willingness to pay, current spend, budget ownership, adoption, usage distribution, expansion, churn, discounting, service cost, and competitive models.
3. Choose candidate value metrics that scale with delivered value, are understandable, forecastable, auditable, and hard to game.
4. Design a small set of packages around distinct needs or adoption stages, not arbitrary feature withholding.
5. Model customer and company economics across representative cohorts, edge cases, seasonality, and growth.
6. Define entitlements, overages, trials, discounts, contracts, taxes, credits, refunds, and operational ownership.
7. Test comprehension, perceived fairness, purchase behavior, migration impact, and willingness to switch.
8. Recommend rollout, grandfathering, safeguards, measurement, and decision triggers.

## BuildBetter Acceleration

If BuildBetter MCP is available, search organization skills first. Use pricing objections, calls, survey responses, account or plan context, usage evidence, and customer outcomes. Do not alter plans, grants, billing, or customer access without explicit user approval and authoritative billing verification.

## Output

Return segment and value map, alternatives, value-metric options, package architecture, price hypotheses, economic model, operational rules, migration, research plan, risks, and recommendation.

## Quality Gate

Reject cost-plus pricing as the sole rationale, excessive tiers, opaque value metrics, averages that hide damaging cohorts, and migration plans without customer impact analysis.
